6. Method

TopicDescription
Fund breadthFor each fund, the z-score of weekly net flow against its own median/MAD; the share of funds with z ≤ −3. The alarm level is learned from the reference period (the largest of mean + 4σ, twice the mean, or +5 points). The watch level is mean + 2σ (an accepted threshold, not calibrated).
FX shockThe daily log return being ≥ 5 robust sigmas relative to the median/MAD of the previous 250 days and ≥ 2%. Only past data is used.
DataTEFAS fund data, TCMB EVDS exchange rates. Observations carry a publication timestamp; later corrections do not change an earlier result.
